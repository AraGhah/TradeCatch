# TradeCatch — 60–90 second product demo script

Use this to record a screen demo (Loom / phone camera on the live site).  
**Do not** invent testimonials, logos, or results in the recording.

**On-site live demo:** homepage hero phone (`#missed-call-demo`) + `#proof-examples`.

---

## English (~75 seconds)

| Time | Visual | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:08 | Homepage hero | “When a Québec plumber or HVAC crew misses a call, that homeowner is already dialling the next company.” |
| 0:08–0:20 | Scroll / play SMS mockup | “TradeCatch texts them back in under thirty seconds — French or English — with messages the contractor approved.” |
| 0:20–0:35 | SMS thread → technician alert card | “They share the address and a photo. The on-call tech gets a full alert — not a vague voicemail.” |
| 0:35–0:50 | Quote follow-up timeline | “If a quote goes quiet, approved follow-ups go out on a schedule and stop the second the customer replies.” |
| 0:50–1:05 | Illustrative dashboard | “The owner sees recovered calls and follow-ups in one place. These numbers are labelled illustrative — not a promise of your results.” |
| 1:05–1:15 | Pilot / audit CTA | “Founding pilot seats are limited. Book a free twenty-minute audit — no system access required.” |

**On-screen lower-third (optional):** `Illustrative product demo · Not a results guarantee`
