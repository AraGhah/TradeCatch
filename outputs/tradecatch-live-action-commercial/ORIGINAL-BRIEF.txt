# TradeCatch — Realistic Live-Action AI Receptionist Commercial

Create a **60–75-second ultra-realistic, cinematic commercial** for TradeCatch, a Canadian SaaS platform that helps contractors answer customer calls, qualify leads, and book appointments automatically using an AI receptionist.

## CRITICAL REQUIREMENT: REAL HUMAN BEINGS

**The video MUST look like it was filmed with real human actors, not animated characters or digital avatars.**

Every person shown must look, move, speak, and behave like a real human being.

- Use photorealistic, live-action footage.
- Show a realistic male contractor, approximately 30–45 years old, working inside a customer's home.
- Show a real-looking customer calling the contractor from another location.
- Use natural facial expressions, realistic body movements, and believable human reactions.
- The people must have realistic skin textures, natural clothing, and authentic environments.
- Use cinematic camera work, natural lighting, and professional commercial production quality.
- Avoid exaggerated acting, robotic movements, artificial facial expressions, or anything resembling an animated advertisement.

**Most importantly, the contractor MUST NOT answer his phone. He is physically occupied with work, ignores the incoming call, and continues working. TradeCatch handles the call automatically without his involvement.**

The AI receptionist should only be heard as a natural, human-sounding voice over the phone. Do not show a robot, virtual assistant avatar, hologram, or artificial human representing the AI.

## Scene 1 — A Contractor Too Busy to Answer (0–12 seconds)

Show a realistic Canadian plumber working underneath a kitchen sink inside a residential home.

He is wearing professional work clothes and using tools to repair a plumbing problem.

His smartphone is resting on the kitchen counter.

Suddenly, his phone starts ringing.

Use a close-up camera shot showing the incoming call.

The contractor briefly looks toward his phone but cannot answer because his hands are occupied with tools.

He returns to his work, allowing the phone to ring unanswered.

**Voiceover (natural, confident male voice):**

"You're busy doing the work. But while you're working, your next customer could be calling."

Show the phone stopping its ringing as the call is automatically handled by TradeCatch.

## Scene 2 — The Customer Calls (12–27 seconds)

Cut to a realistic homeowner sitting in their kitchen, holding their smartphone.

The homeowner has a leaking faucet and is calling the plumbing company for help.

The caller reaches the TradeCatch AI receptionist.

**AI Receptionist (extremely realistic, warm Canadian French female voice):**

"Bonjour! Merci d'avoir appelé Plomberie Martin. For English, press 1."

The homeowner presses 1.

The AI receptionist seamlessly switches to natural English.

**AI Receptionist:**

"Thanks for calling Plomberie Martin! How can I help you today?"

**Customer (natural human voice):**

"Hi! I've got a leaking kitchen faucet. Is there any chance someone could come take a look?"

**AI Receptionist:**

"Of course! Is it leaking constantly, or only when you're using it?"

**Customer:**

"It's been dripping all day."

Make the dialogue sound like an authentic telephone conversation, with natural pacing, realistic pauses, and subtle emotional reactions.

## Scene 3 — TradeCatch Books the Appointment (27–45 seconds)

Show the customer continuing the conversation.

**AI Receptionist:**

"Got it. Let me check our availability. Would tomorrow afternoon work for you?"

**Customer:**

"Yeah, that would be perfect."

**AI Receptionist:**

"We have an opening at 2:30. Does that work?"

**Customer:**

"Absolutely."

**AI Receptionist:**

"Perfect! I'll just need your name and service address."

Show a brief montage of the customer providing their information while the TradeCatch system collects the details.

Transition to an elegant, realistic TradeCatch dashboard interface showing:

- Customer information recorded.
- Plumbing service request created.
- Appointment availability checked.
- Appointment successfully booked.
- Confirmation SMS sent.

**AI Receptionist:**

"You're all booked for tomorrow at 2:30. You'll receive a confirmation text shortly. Have a great day!"

The receptionist must sound genuinely conversational, not like a robotic text-to-speech system.

## Scene 4 — The Contractor Gets a New Job (45–60 seconds)

Cut back to the original contractor.

He is finishing his plumbing repair.

He stands up, washes his hands, and picks up his smartphone.

A TradeCatch notification appears:

"New Appointment Booked"

"Kitchen Faucet Repair"

"Tomorrow — 2:30 PM"

He opens the notification and sees that TradeCatch has automatically handled the customer call and booked the appointment.

Show a natural, subtle smile of satisfaction.

He did not answer the phone, interrupt his work, or manually schedule anything.

**Voiceover:**

"While you were focused on your job, TradeCatch was already booking your next one."

## Scene 5 — Closing Commercial Shot (60–75 seconds)

Show the contractor walking toward his work truck outside the customer's home.

Use a cinematic wide shot with natural afternoon lighting.

Transition smoothly to the TradeCatch logo on a clean background.

**Voiceover:**

"Stop missing calls. Start booking more jobs. Let TradeCatch handle your calls while you handle your business."

**On-screen text:**

TradeCatch

**Your business. Always answering.**

**Book Your Free Demo**

Website: tradecatch.ca

## Production and Visual Requirements

**Visual style:** Premium live-action SaaS commercial, realistic documentary-style cinematography, professional advertising quality.

**People:** Photorealistic human actors. Absolutely no cartoons, animated humans, 3D characters, AI avatars, or robotic figures.

**Camera:** Realistic handheld and stabilized cinematic shots, natural depth of field, close-ups of the phone, tools, customer expressions, and contractor reactions.

**Environment:** Authentic Canadian residential home, believable plumbing equipment, realistic work clothing and smartphones.

**Audio:** Authentic phone ringing, subtle plumbing sounds, realistic telephone audio, natural conversational dialogue, and soft professional background music.

**AI receptionist voice:** Natural Canadian French and North American English, professional and warm, appropriate pauses, subtle intonation, and no mechanical speech patterns.

**Editing:** Smooth scene transitions, modern commercial pacing, natural color grading, and subtle product UI overlays.

**Aspect ratio:** 16:9 for desktop website integration. Keep important visual elements centered so the video can also be adapted to 9:16 for social media.

**Subtitles:** Clean, professionally synchronized subtitles with minimal text overlays.

## Strict Negative Instructions

DO NOT create animated people.

DO NOT use cartoon-style visuals.

DO NOT show a robot or humanoid AI receptionist.

DO NOT show the contractor answering the phone.

DO NOT make the characters look artificial or computer-generated.

DO NOT use exaggerated facial expressions or unnatural body movements.

DO NOT make the video look like a PowerPoint presentation or a generic animated explainer.

DO NOT fill the screen with unnecessary text.

DO NOT use an artificial-sounding narrator.

DO NOT show impossible or unrealistic software interactions.

The finished video should look like a real commercial filmed with professional actors for a serious Canadian technology company.

## Final Goal

The viewer should understand one simple message without requiring any additional explanation:

**A real contractor is too busy working to answer his phone. A real customer calls. TradeCatch's AI receptionist answers, handles the conversation, and books an appointment. The contractor discovers the new booking after finishing his work.**

Make the commercial realistic, emotionally relatable, professional, and persuasive.

The result should make contractors immediately think:

"I miss calls like this all the time. I need this for my business."
