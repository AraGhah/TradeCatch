# TC-004 — Correct notification phone routing

All employee SMS notifications now use the intended member's verified personal
number, including notifications without a configured primary escalation contact.
The dispatcher rechecks verification and number ownership before every send.
Legacy queued business-number jobs are canceled rather than sent to the wrong
destination. Existing delivery idempotency keys remain stable.

Unverified members retain their configured email/in-app notifications; their SMS
is not silently routed to the business number. Verify each employee's phone to
enable SMS. Existing tests now model that prerequisite explicitly.

Validation on October 24, 2026: 107 focused tests passed, zero failures/skips,
including isolated PostgreSQL contact loading, notification recovery and rate
limits, Gmail alerts, receptionist outages, and revoked verification. New cases
exercise multiple recipients with no primary and cancellation of old routing.
