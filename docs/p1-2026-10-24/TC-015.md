# TC-015 — Make notification quotas reliable

Verified October 24, 2026. PostgreSQL dispatch reservations use a transaction and
advisory lock before reading quota usage, claiming rows and inserting reservation
records. Concurrent workers therefore share one organization budget. Claims use
row locks and skip locked work. In-app messages are outside the external budget.

Ordinary dispatches share a forty-message rolling ten-minute budget; critical
messages can additionally use ten reserved dispatches. Unclaimed overflow remains
pending. Expired reservations release capacity, while interrupted processing
leases are recoverable and provider attempt counts remain bounded.

All 15 dedicated quota checks passed with no skips, in memory and isolated
PostgreSQL. They cover simultaneous saturated claims, overlapping workers,
organization isolation, large fan-outs, critical priority/reserve, mixed traffic
and complete eventual draining. No runtime change was necessary for this task.

This verifies the local durable implementation, not live scheduler throughput
or delivery-provider capacity. Those remain operational release checks.
