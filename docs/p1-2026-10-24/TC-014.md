# TC-014 — Detect receptionist outages

Verified and corrected October 24, 2026. Receptionist failures already record a
platform incident and independently queue critical alerts for responsible owners
and admins. Reporting failures do not break the caller's fail-safe response.
Alerts deduplicate within the outage window, support acknowledgment, and respect
configured channels and after-hours rules. Unattributable or suspended-business
failures are recorded without sending business alerts.

Fixed delayed external delivery: outage emails/SMS now recheck the recipient's
enabled outage channels before sending. A disabled channel cancels the queued
delivery. Added memory/PostgreSQL tests with a quiet-hour deferral, an owner
opting out before delivery, and an admin still receiving their enabled channels.
Updated English/French settings guidance to accurately describe verified personal
SMS destinations and after-hours behavior.

All 24 outage tests passed with isolated PostgreSQL and no skips, covering the
call failure path, independent reporting, localization, default/disabled channels,
deduplication and acknowledgment. No real calls or messages were generated.
Live provider availability and delivery require the normal release checks.
