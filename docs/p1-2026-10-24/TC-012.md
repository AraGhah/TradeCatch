# TC-012 — Prevent notifications from being dropped

Verified October 24, 2026. Existing durable outbox and delivery queues already
retain messages when dispatch quotas are exhausted. Only selected deliveries
are claimed; overflow stays pending and is eligible after the window clears.
Critical alerts are processed first and have a ten-dispatch reserve beyond the
ordinary forty external dispatches per organization per ten-minute window.
In-app delivery does not consume the external quota.

The recovery and rate-limit suites verify large recipient fan-outs, overlapping
workers, urgent ordering, the critical reserve, mixed traffic, bounded batches
and eventual draining without message loss. The combined recovery, rate-limit
and outage run passed 79 tests with no skips against isolated PostgreSQL.
No runtime change was needed for this task. Atomic reservation details are
tracked separately in TC-015.

Production scheduler and provider operation were not exercised by these tests.
