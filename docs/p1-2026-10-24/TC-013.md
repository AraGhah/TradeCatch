# TC-013 — Complete daily summaries

Corrected October 24, 2026. Summary scheduling previously used only the business
timezone. Members can now save a personal notification timezone in settings;
blank settings inherit the business timezone. Invalid zones are rejected by the
form, API and service. Existing JSON preference storage persists the setting
without a migration. Both summary scheduling/day boundaries and quiet-hour
deferrals use the personal timezone.

The scheduled job runs every 15 minutes and queues summaries from 18:00 local
time. Per-member/date keys prevent duplicate summaries. Counts include only
activity visible to that member during their local day, with their chosen
English/French language and enabled channels. Quiet days send nothing. Opt-outs
are rechecked during fan-out and dispatch, so previously queued summaries do not
bypass current preferences.

All 34 summary/localization checks passed with isolated PostgreSQL and no skips,
including distinct timezones within one organization, persisted preferences,
local-day counts, invalid zones, opt-out after queueing and daylight-saving
boundaries. Added English/French browser coverage for timezone validation and
save/reload. TypeScript passed; the full CI build and browser suite gate merging.

Live scheduler invocation and real email/SMS delivery remain deployment checks.
