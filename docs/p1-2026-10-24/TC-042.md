# TC-042 — Improve self-service onboarding

Pending product decision, October 25, 2026. Current capability guidance explicitly
defines founder-configured pilot engagements. The owner has been asked whether
to retain assisted onboarding or prepare self-service provisioning and plan
changes. No answer has yet selected the latter.

Retain the current assisted flow while the decision is pending. A self-service
launch requires verified billing entitlements, installation provisioning,
activation prerequisites, failure recovery and real provider acceptance tests.
TC-019, TC-023 and TC-038 remain relevant release dependencies. No production
onboarding or billing-portal policy was changed.
