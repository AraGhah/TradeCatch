# TC-011 — Recover failed SMS notifications

Verified and corrected October 24, 2026. Signed Twilio final status callbacks
already distinguish transient failures (bounded retries) from permanent failures,
preserve delivered status against older callbacks, and recover through a durable
backup-email queue with stable keys. Worker retries recover interrupted bookkeeping.

Fixed a permission gap: recovery now checks the fallback recipient's category
email preference before queueing. Before sending, it rechecks the preference,
enabled recovery rule and selected backup. Revoked permissions cancel the queued
delivery; the failure alert remains available. Membership and destination checks
continue to prevent delivery to removed or changed recipients.

Added memory/PostgreSQL cases for disabled recipient email preferences and
preference, rule or recipient changes after queueing. The combined recovery,
rate-limit and outage suites passed all 79 tests with no skips on isolated local
PostgreSQL. Providers were simulated; no real messages were sent.

Provider reference: https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/api/message-resource .
Live Twilio callback configuration and real carrier delivery remain release checks.
