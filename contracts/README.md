## Lawyer review checklist

Ask counsel to review and adapt at least:

1. Master Service Agreement (MSA)
2. Statement of Work (SOW) template
3. Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
4. Service-Level Expectations (Schedule A)
5. Payment and cancellation terms (MSA § fees / termination)
6. Limitation-of-liability provisions (MSA)
7. Client responsibility for technician availability (MSA)
8. Emergency-handling disclaimer (MSA + Schedule C)
9. Authorization to connect phone, email, calendar, and CRM (Schedule C)
10. Authorization to communicate with the contractor’s customers (Schedule C)
11. Subprocessor disclosure (Schedule B + DPA)
12. Data-retention and deletion terms (DPA)
13. Incident-notification procedure (DPA)

## Non-negotiable product statements (must appear clearly)

TradeCatch:

- Does **not** diagnose plumbing, electrical, HVAC, medical, or safety problems
- Does **not** provide emergency instructions unless the contractor supplied and approved them
- Does **not** guarantee revenue
- Does **not** guarantee that every caller will respond
- Does **not** replace licensed technicians
- **Requires** the contractor to maintain valid on-call contacts
- **Requires** the contractor to approve messages, escalation rules, and service areas

1. Lawyer reviews and finalizes language (EN + FR as advised).

4. Keep signed PDFs off this repo (or in a private vault). Do not commit client-signed copies with personal information.
