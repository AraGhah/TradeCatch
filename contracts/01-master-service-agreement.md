# Master Service Agreement (MSA)

**Client:** `[CLIENT]` (“Client”, “you”)

This Master Service Agreement, together with each Statement of Work (“SOW”), the Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”), and Schedules A–C (collectively, the “Agreement”), governs TradeCatch’s design, installation, and management of automated communication workflows for the Client’s trade business.

---

## 1. Services

1.1 TradeCatch provides professional services and managed systems that may include: missed-call detection and SMS text-back; lead information collection; technician or owner alerts; quote follow-up sequences; dashboards or CRM-related logging; bilingual (French/English) message templates; testing; and ongoing monitoring as described in an applicable SOW.

1.2 Services are scoped only in a signed SOW. Work outside a SOW requires a written change order.

1.3 TradeCatch may use subprocessors listed in Schedule B (as updated under the DPA).

---

## 2. What TradeCatch does not do (critical limitations)

**The Client acknowledges and agrees that TradeCatch:**

(a) **Does not diagnose** plumbing, electrical, HVAC, technical, medical, or safety problems;  
(b) **Does not provide emergency instructions** to end customers unless those instructions were **supplied and approved in writing by the Client** before use;  
(c) **Does not guarantee revenue**, bookings, lead volume, conversion rates, or any particular business result;  
(d) **Does not guarantee that every caller or quote recipient will respond**;  
(e) **Does not replace licensed technicians**, dispatchers, or the Client’s professional judgment;  
(f) **Does not replace 911 or public emergency services**; and  
(g) is not a phone carrier, CRM vendor, or insurer.

Results depend on factors outside TradeCatch’s control, including demand, pricing, reputation, staffing, message quality approved by the Client, and third-party network or software availability.

---

## 3. Client responsibilities

The Client shall:

3.1 **Approve before go-live** all customer-facing messages, escalation rules, service areas, hours of operation, emergency-handling language, and routing contacts.

3.2 **Maintain valid on-call contacts** (phone numbers and backup contacts) that TradeCatch may alert under approved rules, and update TradeCatch promptly when contacts change.

3.3 Ensure licensed and qualified personnel handle diagnosis, pricing, safety decisions, and all emergency response.

3.4 Provide accurate business information and timely access needed to connect approved systems (see Schedule C).

3.5 Comply with applicable law for contacting customers (including Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), Québec privacy Law 25, and telecom rules), and only request communications that the Client is authorized to send.

3.6 Keep its own emergency-response process independent of TradeCatch.

Failure to maintain on-call contacts or approved rules may cause missed or delayed escalations for which TradeCatch is not responsible.

---

## 4. Authorizations

By signing this Agreement (and Schedule C), the Client authorizes TradeCatch to:

4.1 Connect and configure the Client’s **phone, email, calendar, and CRM** (or equivalent) accounts as reasonably required to deliver the SOW; and  
4.2 **Communicate with the Client’s customers** on the Client’s behalf using only Client-approved templates and rules (SMS, email, or other channels specified in the SOW).

Authorizations are detailed in **Schedule C**. The Client remains the controller of its customer relationships and personal information (see DPA).

---

## 5. Emergency handling

5.1 TradeCatch systems escalate using **Client-approved rules only**. TradeCatch does not independently assess whether a situation is an emergency.

5.2 Any emergency-related wording in templates must make clear, if applicable, that life-threatening situations require contacting public emergency services first.

5.3 Handling of urgent or safety-related situations remains **solely the Client’s responsibility**.

---

## 6. Fees, payment, cancellation, and refunds

6.1 **Fees.** Setup and monthly management fees are set out in the SOW and invoiced as stated there.

6.2 **Payment.** Invoices are due within `[NET 15 / NET 30]` days unless the SOW says otherwise. Late amounts may accrue interest at the maximum rate permitted by Québec law, and TradeCatch may suspend services after written notice for non-payment.

6.3 **Setup fees.** Once design, configuration, message drafting, testing, or launch work under a SOW has begun, setup fees are **non-refundable**.

6.4 **Monthly management.** Either party may terminate ongoing management with written notice of `[30]` days (or the period in the SOW). Service continues through the notice period unless both parties agree otherwise.

6.5 **Prepaid management.** Unused prepaid management fees for a notice period are not refunded except as required by applicable Québec law.

6.6 **Founding / launch credits.** Any founding-rate or launch-guarantee credit applies only if expressly stated in the SOW.

Availability and response targets are described in **Schedule A (Service-Level Expectations)**. Schedule A sets operational expectations; it is **not** a warranty of uninterrupted service and does not create liquidated damages unless a SOW expressly says so.

## 8. Intellectual property

8.1 TradeCatch retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, workflows, software, and know-how.  
8.2 Upon full payment of setup fees, the Client receives a non-exclusive licence to use Client-approved message content and configurations for its own business during the term.  
8.3 Client data and customer personal information remain the Client’s (see DPA).

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other’s confidential business information with reasonable care and use it only to perform the Agreement, except for information that is public, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law.

---

## 10. Personal information

Processing of personal information is governed by the **DPA**. In case of conflict on privacy/security topics, the DPA controls.

## 11. Warranties

11.1 TradeCatch will perform services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry practice for similar small-business automation services.

11.2 **Disclaimer.** Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, services are provided **as is**. TradeCatch disclaims all other warranties, including merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, and non-infringement, to the fullest extent permitted by Québec law.

## 12. Limitation of liability

12.1 To the maximum extent permitted by applicable Québec law, neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, punitive, or exemplary damages, or for lost profits, lost revenue, lost data, or business interruption, even if advised of the possibility.

12.2 TradeCatch’s aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement will not exceed the **fees paid by the Client to TradeCatch under the applicable SOW in the twelve (12) months** before the claim (or, for setup-only claims before management begins, the setup fees paid under that SOW).

12.3 TradeCatch is not liable for failures or delays caused by: the Client’s missing or outdated on-call contacts; Client-unapproved or Client-delayed approvals; third-party carriers, SMS gateways, email providers, phone systems, CRMs, or calendars; force majeure; or the Client’s misuse of the system.

12.4 Nothing in this Agreement excludes liability that cannot be limited under Québec public order rules (as determined by a court of competent jurisdiction).

---

## 13. Indemnity

13.1 **Client.** The Client will defend and indemnify TradeCatch against claims arising from: Client message content; Client instructions; Client failure to maintain on-call contacts; Client’s services to end customers; or Client’s violation of law (including CASL and privacy law), except to the extent caused by TradeCatch’s gross negligence or willful misconduct.

13.2 **TradeCatch.** TradeCatch will defend and indemnify the Client against third-party claims that TradeCatch-provided tooling (excluding Client content and third-party platforms) infringes Canadian IP rights, subject to the liability cap in §12.

---

## 14. Term and termination

14.1 This MSA starts on the Effective Date and continues until terminated. Individual SOWs have their own term.

14.2 Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within fifteen (15) days after written notice (or immediately for non-payment after the notice period in §6.2).

14.3 On termination, TradeCatch will stop new automated sends and assist with reasonable transition as set out in the DPA (deletion/return). Setup fees remain earned as in §6.3.
