# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

**DRAFT — FOR QUÉBEC LAWYER REVIEW. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.**  
Aligns with Québec Law 25 (Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector) and, where applicable, PIPEDA. Counsel should confirm roles (controller / processor / “person carrying on an enterprise”) and cross-border wording.

**Effective date:** `[EFFECTIVE DATE]`  
**Parties:** TradeCatch (“Processor” / service provider) and `[CLIENT]` (“Client” / enterprise responsible for the personal information of its customers and staff as applicable).  
This DPA forms part of the MSA. If conflict on privacy/security: **this DPA prevails**.

---

## 1. Subject matter and roles

1.1 TradeCatch processes personal information **on behalf of the Client** to deliver services under the MSA/SOW (e.g. phone numbers, names, addresses, message content, job photos, CRM identifiers).

1.2 The Client determines the purposes of collection and remains responsible for a lawful basis to contact end customers (including CASL consent where required) and for transparency toward individuals.

1.3 TradeCatch processes personal information only on documented Client instructions (the MSA, SOW, approved templates/rules, and this DPA), unless required by law.

---

## 2. Categories of data and data subjects

| Category (examples) | Data subjects |
| --- | --- |
| Identity & contact | Client staff; Client customers / leads |
| Communication content | SMS/email bodies, photos submitted by leads |
| Operational metadata | Timestamps, delivery status, assignment status |
| Account credentials / tokens | Only as needed to connect Client systems (Schedule C) |

Special categories / sensitive data: Client must not instruct TradeCatch to collect health diagnoses or unnecessary sensitive data. Job photos may incidentally show interiors; Client remains responsible for necessity and minimization.

---

## 3. TradeCatch obligations

TradeCatch shall:

3.1 Process personal information only for performing the SOW and related support.  
3.2 Ensure persons authorized to process data are bound by confidentiality.  
3.3 Implement technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk (encryption in transit, access restriction, logging of admin access, secure development practices).  
3.4 Not sell personal information.  
3.5 Assist the Client, insofar as possible, with individual requests (access, correction, deletion) that relate to data TradeCatch stores.  
3.6 Notify the Client of personal-information incidents as set out in §7.  
3.7 Delete or return personal information on termination as set out in §6, except where retention is required by law or for dispute/billing records about the Client relationship (not end-customer marketing databases).

---

## 4. Client obligations

The Client shall:

4.1 Provide only personal information necessary for the services.  
4.2 Ensure notices/consents required by Law 25, CASL, and other laws are in place before instructing TradeCatch to message individuals.  
4.3 Approve message content and retention-related settings where the product allows.  
4.4 Maintain accurate on-call staff contacts and promptly request removal of stale personal information of its own staff when employment ends.

---

## 5. Subprocessors

5.1 Client authorizes TradeCatch to use subprocessors in **Schedule B**.  
5.2 TradeCatch will impose data-protection terms no less protective than this DPA in substance.  
5.3 TradeCatch will give the Client reasonable advance notice of material subprocessor changes (email sufficient). Client may object on reasonable privacy grounds; parties will discuss alternatives in good faith. If no agreement, Client may terminate the affected SOW for convenience with the MSA notice period (setup fees remain non-refundable as earned).  
5.4 International transfers (if any) will use contractual or other mechanisms counsel approves (e.g. contractual clauses + assessment under Law 25).

---

## 6. Retention and deletion

6.1 **Operational message/lead logs:** retained for `[12 / 24]` months after creation unless the SOW sets a shorter period or Client requests earlier deletion where technically feasible.  
6.2 **Client account / configuration:** retained for the term of the SOW + `[90]` days.  
6.3 **Backups:** cycled on provider schedules; residual deletion follows backup expiry.  
6.4 **On termination:** within `[30]` days after Client’s written request, TradeCatch will delete or return Client-held end-customer personal information under its control, except: (a) data the Client still holds in its own CRM/phone systems; (b) anonymized/aggregated statistics; (c) records TradeCatch must keep for legal, tax, or dispute purposes (limited to Client billing/identity, not full customer message histories unless required).  
6.5 Client may export available data before termination on request during the notice period.

---

## 7. Incident-notification procedure

7.1 **Definition.** A “Confidentiality Incident” means unauthorized access, use, or disclosure of personal information, loss of personal information, or any other breach of personal-information confidentiality, as contemplated under Law 25.

7.2 **Internal detection.** TradeCatch will investigate suspected incidents without undue delay.

7.3 **Notice to Client.** TradeCatch will notify the Client’s privacy contact at `[CLIENT PRIVACY EMAIL]` **as soon as possible**, and in any event within **seventy-two (72) hours** of confirming a Confidentiality Incident involving personal information processed for the Client, unless a shorter period is required by law or the incident poses a risk of serious injury requiring faster notice.

7.4 **Content of notice** (as known at the time): nature of the incident; categories of data and approximate number of individuals affected; likely consequences; measures taken or proposed; point of contact.

7.5 **CAI / individuals.** The Client is generally responsible for notifications to the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec (CAI) and to individuals when Law 25 requires it. TradeCatch will reasonably cooperate and will not notify individuals or the CAI on the Client’s behalf unless legally required to do so or expressly instructed in writing by the Client.

7.6 **Coordination.** Neither party will make public statements naming the other without prior consultation, except as required by law.

---

## 8. Security audits

Upon reasonable written notice (no more than once per 12 months unless an incident occurred), Client may request a summary of TradeCatch’s relevant security practices or a written questionnaire response. On-site audits only if required by law or after a confirmed incident, at Client’s expense unless TradeCatch is at fault.

---

## 9. Liability

Liability arising from this DPA is subject to the MSA’s limitation of liability, except where prohibited by Québec public order.

---

## 10. Term

This DPA lasts for as long as TradeCatch processes personal information for the Client under the MSA/SOW.
