## 2. Authorization to communicate with the Client’s customers

2.1 The Client authorizes TradeCatch to send and receive communications **as agent of the Client** to individuals who contact or are contacted in connection with the Client’s trade business, through channels enabled in the SOW (SMS, email, or others).

2.2 Communications must follow **only** templates, timing, escalation rules, and service areas the Client has approved in writing.

2.3 The Client represents that it has all rights and consents required by law (including CASL and Québec privacy law) to authorize these communications.

2.4 The Client remains the identified sender / business responsible for the customer relationship. TradeCatch is a technical facilitator, not the trade contractor.

---

## 3. On-call and technician availability

3.1 The Client will keep a current list of on-call and backup contacts and is solely responsible for technician availability, licensing, and response to alerts.

3.2 TradeCatch is not responsible if alerts are sent correctly under approved rules but no technician answers, is unavailable, or declines the job.

---

## 4. Emergency-handling acknowledgement

By signing, the Client acknowledges that:

- TradeCatch does **not** diagnose plumbing, electrical, HVAC, medical, or safety problems;  
- TradeCatch does **not** provide emergency instructions unless the Client supplied and approved them;  
- TradeCatch does **not** replace 911 / public emergency services or licensed technicians;  
- The Client must maintain its own emergency-response process.

---

## 5. Duration and revocation

These authorizations last for the SOW term and any wind-down period. Client may revoke access by written notice; revocation may prevent TradeCatch from performing the services and does not entitle the Client to a setup-fee refund once work has begun.
