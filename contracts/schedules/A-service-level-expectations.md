# Schedule A — Service-Level Expectations (SLE)

**DRAFT — FOR QUÉBEC LAWYER REVIEW. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.**  
Attached to the MSA. These are **operational expectations**, not a guarantee of revenue, caller response rates, or uninterrupted third-party networks.

---

## 1. Scope

Applies to managed services during a paid monthly management term under an active SOW.

---

## 2. Automated system behaviour (targets)

| Expectation | Target |
| --- | --- |
| First Client-approved SMS after a detected missed call | Typically under **30 seconds** when telephony/SMS providers are operating normally |
| Quote follow-up sends | Per the Client-approved schedule |
| Stop follow-ups on customer reply | As soon as a reply is detected by the connected channel |

Delays caused by carriers, SMS gateways, Client phone configuration, or force majeure do not breach this Schedule.

---

## 3. TradeCatch human support (business hours)

**Business hours:** Monday–Friday, `[9:00–17:00]` America/Toronto, excluding Québec statutory holidays, unless the SOW says otherwise.

| Request type | Response target |
| --- | --- |
| General configuration / monitoring question | Within **1 business day** |
| Suspected outage of TradeCatch-managed automation | Within **4 business hours** during business hours; best effort after hours |
| Message/rule change request | Queued and applied after Client written approval; timing agreed case by case |

---

## 4. Monitoring

TradeCatch will periodically review that configured workflows are running and will notify the Client of material failures it detects. The Client remains responsible for verifying on-call phone reachability.

---

## 5. Maintenance and changes

Planned maintenance that may interrupt automation will be notice-emailed when reasonably practicable. Emergency security patches may occur without prior notice.

---

## 6. Exclusions

No SLE credit or breach applies for: Client-approved downtime; Client failure to maintain on-call contacts; Client delay in approvals; misuse; third-party platform outages; or events beyond reasonable control.

---

## 7. Remedies

Unless a SOW expressly provides service credits, the Client’s remedy for SLE misses is commercially reasonable remediation and, if material and uncured, termination rights under the MSA—not damages beyond the MSA liability cap.
