# TradeCatch · one page

**Verdict: Not yet.** Year 1 loses money at the placeholder founder wage; the buyer-panel check passes. Written 2026-11-15.

**The problem.** A plumber, HVAC or roofing owner on a job site cannot answer the phone. The caller rings the next company on Google. A quote sent and never chased goes quiet.

**The customer.** Owner-operated trade businesses of 1 to 10 people in Montréal, Laval and nearby, whose owner answers a personal cell on the job. First target: plumbing and HVAC.

**The offer and price (CAD).** A bilingual (Québec French and English) AI receptionist that answers in the contractor's name, captures name, number, address, need and urgency, texts the owner right away, chases quiet quotes (day 1, 3, 7, 14), and can connect an urgent call when the owner presses 1. The founder installs, tests and watches it for each client.

- Essential: $1,999.99 setup, then $249 a month.
- Professional: $1,999.99 setup, then $349 a month; also books into Google Calendar.
- Proposed launch offer: a 60-day refund of the setup fee if it has not captured 10 real caller requests, a free call-log count in the demo, and the first 5 installs only.

**The numbers** (from the plan; projections with estimates marked in `numbers.json`):

| key number | |
| --- | ---: |
| Price | $299.00 a client-month |
| Profit margin at plan | -4% per client-month |
| Break-even | 13 client-months a day |
| Year 1 operating profit | $-13,687 |
| Startup spend | $2,000 |
| Cash needed before it pays for itself | $15,687 |
| Startup money earned back | not in year 1 |
| Buyer panel | 6 buy · 14 pass |

"13 client-months a day" means 13 active clients. Each client-month leaves $182.92 (61%) after its own costs. The year-1 loss comes from a **placeholder $4,000 a month founder wage**; at $2,500 year 1 is +$4,313, and with no wage drawn it is profitable from the first client.

**The evidence.**

- **Buyer panel (simulated):** 6 of 20 buy (30%) after the offer was rebuilt, against 1 of 20 (5%) before. 30% is an upper bound.
- **Competitors:** none of ten bilingual vendors in `competitors.md` shows a customer count, a rating or a recorded Québec French call. Only two install for you, at $797 a month. None publishes a setup fee, so the $1,999.99 must be earned by proof.
- **Board (simulated experts):** 3 "fund if", 0 fund, 0 pass, average 5.3 of 10.
- **Real test:** none yet. The product is built and in production; no paying client is recorded.

**What the money is for.** The founder's runway until break-even, $2,000 of legal and accounting set-up, and the guarantee exposure (about $400 per new client at a 20% claim rate).

**The ask.** *Founder to complete: amount, terms and use.* What the numbers support: cash of $15,687 at the placeholder wage, $3,489 at a $2,500 wage, $2,000 if no wage is drawn. The ask can only be sized once the founder sets the real pay and times a real install.

**The first test.** Three paid pilot clients in plumbing or HVAC within 45 days of launch (default launch Monday 2026-12-07), at least one by referral. If fewer than 2 of the first 20 demos sign, the offer is revisited before any spend.

*The buyer panel is simulated buyers and the numbers are projections; real customers and real quotes confirm them. Not financial, legal or tax advice.*
