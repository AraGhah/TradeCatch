# Buyer panel: TradeCatch (a bilingual AI phone receptionist for Québec contractors)

**1 buy · 19 pass** (5% buy) out of 20 simulated buyers. Seed 7, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- Calling the demo line myself, in French, as an angry customer with an urgent problem, and having it sound natural and text me the right details, plus being able to try it a month before paying the full setup fee.
- Two or three Montréal or Laval trade owners I can actually call who use it in both languages, or a paid trial month where the setup fee is waived or refunded if it doesn't catch jobs I would have missed.
- Drop the setup fee, or let me pay it only after the first month if it actually brings in a job I would have lost.
- Talking to two or three roofers or landscapers around Laval who have used it for a full season and can tell me how many jobs it got them, or a first month where I don't pay the setup unless it proves itself.
- Proof from local trades with real numbers, or a trial month with the setup fee waived or refundable if it doesn't catch calls I would have missed.
- A trial month without the $2,000 setup, or the setup refunded if it doesn't catch at least one job I'd have missed, after I hear it handle my own test calls in Québec French.
- If the setup was waived or refunded when it doesn't bring me at least one real job in the first two months, I'd try it.
- Leads and bookings landing straight in my existing CRM, plus a trial month or a smaller setup fee so I can see the booked jobs it catches before paying $2,000.
- A trial on my real line for a few weeks with no setup fee, where I can read every call transcript and see it handle bilingual callers without mistakes.
- If it pushed the caller's details and bookings straight into my existing scheduling/CRM tool, with the setup fee cut or credited back after a trial month where I see it catch real jobs.
- Drop the setup fee, or give me a free month on my real line so I can count the jobs it actually catches that I would have lost.
- Calling the demo line in French and English and having it sound like a real Montréal receptionist in both, plus a written privacy policy saying where the data is stored, and two or three local trade owners I can call who have used it for months.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
